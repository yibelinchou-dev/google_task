## Vision
PreFlight is a guided pre-build demand & distribution toolkit for vibe-coded indie SaaS founders: ICP interviews, waitlist smoke tests, channel experiments, and a kill/continue score before another feature dump. North star: share of started experiments that produce an explicit kill or continue decision with evidence before more coding.

## Core features (6)
- **ICP interview tracker**: Guided interview scripts, note capture, and evidence tags feeding the kill-score.
- **Waitlist smoke-test kit**: Landing copy templates, email capture, paid-intent checkbox, and threshold dashboard.
- **Channel experiment board**: Non-spam distribution experiments (Show HN sequencing, adopter interviews, star→user conversion) with outcome logging.
- **Kill/continue score engine**: Transparent rubric combining interviews, waitlist metrics, and channel evidence into kill / continue / more-data.
- **Concierge demand sprint booking**: Optional $99 2-week sprint intake for founders who want human-guided validation.
- **Static clickable demo (PreFlight UI)**: Polished single-page demo for Spacefast private publish and interview walkthroughs.
